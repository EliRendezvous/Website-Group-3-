ABOUT THE GAME

Corruption Protocol is a fast-paced 2D action-survival experience where survival is a constant battle against overwhelming forces and a growing internal corruption. Set in a dark and mysterious world, players must fight through relentless waves of enemies while completing structured objectives that test their movement, timing, and control.

As you progress, a unique corruption system steadily builds, increasing the intensity of the game and ultimately summoning the deadly Stalker—a powerful entity that relentlessly hunts you down. What begins as a fight for survival quickly escalates into a high-stakes confrontation.

With progressive difficulty, objective-based gameplay, and fast-paced combat, Corruption Protocol delivers a tense and replayable experience where the longer you survive… the more dangerous everything becomes.
